# Future level ideas

Levels players have asked for, or that we have thought of, which are not built yet. Nothing here is a promise or a plan: each idea needs its own plan (like the ones in [new-levels/](new-levels/)) before work starts.

## Level 73: The Redlands

Asked for on the mod database (October 2026).

**Lore** (Wikidot, [Level 73](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-73), survival difficulty 5):
- **The island:** one medium-sized island of dark red-black ground in an unexplored, dangerous ocean.
- **The sky:** a full moon fixed overhead, often hidden by thick black clouds, which plunge the island into darkness. Artificial light is discouraged.
- **The Silver Castle:** a gothic castle on the highest point, with stone walls, a gatehouse and a path to its door. It has three floors and an attic, with curtained and boarded windows. Inside are a main hall, a dining hall, guest bedrooms and a master bedroom. Food and water appear on the dining table, and a fireplace gives heat. Its paintings show the Frontrooms but lead nowhere.
- **The island is swarming:** about one entity per 3.3 m², hunting in groups. The wiki lists Windows, Smilers, Deathmoths, Clumps, Dullers, Hounds, Skin-Stealers, Death Rats and Camo Crawlers. The advice is to run straight for the castle.
- **CORAN:** a blind, 7'9" entity with three regenerating hearts. He attacks other entities but not people, and leads Coran's Children, the 37 survivors living in the castle.
- **The way in and out:** entered from Level 6, Level 9.1 (near the Gloom) and Level 14. There are no known exits.

**Why it fits:** it is canon on the wiki the mod is built from, and it is entered from Level 6, which is already in the mod. Much of it can reuse what exists:
- Level 7's sea and islands;
- Level 94's castle planning;
- the Suburbs' night sky and atmosphere blending;
- the menace director and several of its entities: Hounds, Deathmoths, Death Rats and Smilers where we have them.

**Open questions:**
- **No exits:** a one-way trap only makes sense if waking up on Level 0 is the way out, as it is elsewhere.
- **Entity density:** the wiki's crowd would need its own director rules: a "run to the castle" level rather than a stalk-and-hunt one.
- **CORAN:** whether he is a neutral NPC, a protector, or only lore in notes.
- **The castle:** whether it becomes a sanctuary with renewable food at the dining table, and how that fits the scarcity setting.

## Infinite IKEA (SCP-3008)

Asked for on the mod database (October 2026), with a second player agreeing; the first player later added that it "better be a separate mod with its own progression".

**Probably not a fit for this mod.** SCP-3008 belongs to the SCP Foundation canon, not the Backrooms wikis. Every level in the mod is adapted from the Backrooms wikis, and keeping to that lore is a deliberate choice. An IKEA level would be the one place that breaks it.

**Better as a separate mod** with its own world type and progression:
- the store's endless aisles and showrooms;
- the "staff" who turn hostile when the lights go out;
- building forts from flat-pack furniture;
- the food court as the food source.

It could share code with this mod (the layout harness, the lighting, the menace director), but it would need its own content and survival loop.

Note: we told players on the mod page it would be "added soon". Whatever is decided, a reply saying where it is going (a separate mod, or not for now) would be fair to them.
