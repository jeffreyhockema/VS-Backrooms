# Needs: the user's decisions

Made 2026-10-24 before any code, each from a short list of options with a recommendation. The recommended option
is marked (rec.).

1. **Old worlds** (made before this update, no setting stored): **both needs on** (rec.), meters start full, a
   one-time chat note says how to turn one off with `/worldconfig`. This is a stated exception to the project rule
   that a new setting's default gives the old behaviour.
2. **Sleep passes time by the vanilla rule** (rec.): everyone in bed makes the clock run fast; a lone player on a
   server waits for the others.
3. **Sleeping places: every bed-like block plus a craftable carpet bedroll** (rec.; the user's words: "beds +
   carpet bedroll?"). No sleeping on the bare floor.
4. **Exhaustion hurts sanity and draws entities only** (rec.): no direct damage, no micro-sleeps. The Backrooms
   kill you, not the meter.
5. **Water supply: one-time stock plus rare seeps** (rec.): coolers, pumps, fountains and carts never restock;
   Level 8 seeps and Level 10 ponds stay renewable; Level 974's taps stay; boxes keep their Almond Water.
6. **World water is drinkable, by risk class** (rec.): clean (Level 8 pools, Level 10 ponds, Level 37 pools, 974),
   tainted (Level 1 to 5 puddles, house pools, city water, flooded houses: half hydration, a stability hit, a
   chance of sickness), salt (Level 7 sea dehydrates). Empty hands, hold right-click one second.
7. **Thirst runs like hunger; a bottle is 40%** (rec.): full to empty in about 25 real minutes of walking, idle far
   slower; Almond Water hydrates 40% and gives no satiety; below 40% you slow, at empty slow damage like starving.
8. **About one real hour awake before fatigue bites** (rec.): rested about 48 minutes, drowsy (can sleep) from
   there, exhausted at about 90 minutes, dead on your feet at about 2 hours.
9. **Night visits** (rec.): sleeping outside a safe zone rolls, per slept hour, a creature placed nearby and
   hunting, scaled by the level's danger; shut doors near the bed halve the odds; the sleeper wakes as it closes in.
10. **Bottles and boiling** (rec.): drinking leaves an empty bottle; fill it at any water; murky water boils clean in
    a firepit (the second recipe); salt water never does.
11. **Heat drains thirst instead of hunger** (rec.) while thirst is on; with thirst off it drains hunger as today.
12. **Blue and Green Almond Water now** (not the recommendation, which was "later"): Blue hydrates, restores extra
    stability and lets you sleep at once; Green hydrates and holds fatigue off for a while, then it lands at once.
    Red stays for later.

Earlier decisions this plan overturns: "No thirst for now" (2026-10-04) and "Almond Water is a plain food item"
(2026-10-04), both the user's at the time.
