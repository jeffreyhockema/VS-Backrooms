# Level 23 "The Petrified Garden": lore brief and build plan

## 0. The wikis do NOT describe the same level
- Wikidot https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-23, "The Petrified Garden" (Kitty Rika and Beep Bot): the planetoid-sized tree superorganism. Difficulty "4?", tags Unsafe, Overgrown, Medium Entity Count. All lore below is from it.
- Fandom https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Level_23 is "Viennese Sunday": Class 2, an endless quasi-replica of Vienna's historic centre, cobbled streets, baroque facades, fixed daytime, 10-27 C, fog, newspapers that predict history, GIS inspection vans, a sewer mining base. A search of its wikitext for petrified, garden, planetoid and tree finds nothing relevant, and a Fandom search for "Petrified Garden" returned nothing. So the premise that Fandom's text also calls it the Petrified Garden is not borne out. **Choice: follow Wikidot only.** Nothing from Vienna is used.

## 1. Lore (Wikidot; confidence good, the page is clear but short)
- **What it is.** "a massive, planetoid-sized superorganism comprised primarily of several intertwining tree specimens". Earth-like gravity; surface area about that of Ceres. "Travel throughout the Level is possible due to pockets of air and winding tunnels inside the Level's surface, similar to a large cave system."
- **Surface.** "an extremely dense forest that lets little to no light through." No day/night cycle; a star in the sky that never moves; the planetoid is "permanently bathed in sunlight from all directions". Tree species hugely varied: Douglas fir, aspen, mahogany, redwood, cedar, cypress, white oak, exotic and extinct ones, some unknown.
- **Glow rooms** (underground): large caverns, "like underground forests", lit by Gardener's Sorries (bioluminescent, glowworm-like, many colours, always whispering "sorry"), glowing fungi and bushes, damp cool air, the most entities.
- **Ancient ruins** deep in glow rooms, overgrown with moss and fungi: a pyramidal Lighthouse of Alexandria, a spherical Great Pyramid, a square Colosseum, a triangular Parthenon. Pottery (most common), damaged paintings, cloth, all worse than the originals; hasty messages in Latin; nothing later than 1000 AD. Plaque at the Great Sphere, translated: "We dedicate this Great Sphere of Giza to the Great Gardener / In hopes of forgiveness for the great wrong we have committed / Already our purple eyes have turned green ... Our only hope is that, alone and petrified, we will not disappear / As Architects of a failed project."
- **Buried sentries**: trapped in wood and bark; saying "it's only a matter of time" makes them scream, "like rusty chainsaws cutting wood", loud enough to burst eardrums.
- **Heart of Water / core**: hollow, under a spherical ceiling held up by Green Giants' trunks; a huge central reservoir feeds the whole level; trees on the ceiling condense vapour and pipe it to the glow rooms.
- **Green Giants**: up to ~3.2 km tall and 18.3 m wide, must not be tampered with.
- **Entities**: Volpes, Hounds, Clumps, Smilers, Death Rats, Deathmoths, Curabitur Birds, unknown animal-like natives in glow rooms; Wranglers very rarely.
- **Base Seedling** (M.E.G.): in a glow room; ~30 members plus 5 rescued wanderers; friendly, trades; Almond Water squirt guns; area cleared and free of sentries; limited food, steady water; guards the only consistent exit.
- **Entrances**: huge hollow green-barked trees in Levels 37 and 47; a hollow tree in Level 135 (to Base Seedling); a hollow log in Level 121. **Exits**: enter the core reservoir and be noclipped into the ocean of Level 7 or 121 (or an exhibit in Level 43); a hollow tree in Base Seedling returns to Level 135.
- Not stated: temperature beyond "damp and cool" in glow rooms, any sound beyond the whispers and screams, food sources. Anything on those below is my suggestion.

## 2. Build plan in this mod

**Scale reading (my design).** A planet cannot be modelled. The region (a few hundred blocks, open sky, 32-block cells) is a patch of the planet's crust: the open sky is the canopy lid, the walkable layer is the forest floor and root maze, glow rooms are caverns carved beneath, and the Heart of Water is a hollow basin at one point. Petrified wood and vanilla stone/log blocks can carry most of it.

**Must**
- Open-sky region (as Level 73), band ~60 blocks. Canopy: a leaf/dark-green lid at ~+50 with a few sun holes so the sky reads as thick leaves with a fixed star glow. Surface: trunks at jittered lattice points per cell (4-14 wide, 20-50 high, petrified wood or log blocks), buttress roots arching between neighbours, humus/moss/leaf-litter ground with root ridges; walkable gaps 3-6 wide so it reads dense and dim.
- Glow rooms: caverns at depth -14 to -2 (40-100 blocks across), entered by sloping root tunnels, with miniature forests and glow fungi, hash-placed per cell.
- Core: a hollow basin ringed by giant trunks (Green Giants, 12-18 wide, passing through the canopy) around a water pool; a landmark visible from afar.
- Blocks/textures: petrified wood (grey grain), bark, huge trunk variants, root, moss/humus, several leaf tints, glow fungus (emissive), mushroom cap, mossy ancient stone, wet earth; reuse vanilla stone, logs, water.
- Light: surface very dim green (sky light ~4-6, green fog), no day cycle; glow rooms soft coloured lights (teal, violet, amber); a few bright star-shafts through canopy holes.
- Sound bed: creaking wood, far groans, drips and trickle, leaf rustle, cave echo, whispered "sorry" near Sorry lights, rare distant sentry scream.
- Entities (existing): Hound, Death Rat, Clump, Smiler, Deathmoth, medium count, concentrated in glow rooms. Volpe, Curabitur Bird, Wrangler, natives: skip for the first pass.
- Loot: wood, foraged fungus, clean water at the core pools, pottery shards and cloth in ruins. Edible fungus is my invention (lore names no food).

**Should**
- Base Seedling: a safe glow-room pocket (no hostile spawns), M.E.G. tents, squirt-gun prop, a hollow-tree prop, trade-style notes.
- Ruins: four wrong-shaped models (pyramidal lighthouse, spherical pyramid, square Colosseum, triangular Parthenon) in mossy stone, pottery props, Latin wall plates, the plaque at the Great Sphere.
- Sorries as small coloured light props with the whisper sound.
- Buried sentries: face/mouth bark wall variants; optional chat-phrase scream hook.

**Nice**
- High branch walkways; exotic flora set pieces; falling leaf/spore particles; native animals; Curabitur Birds.

**Wanderer note ideas** (mine, from lore hooks)
1. "A hollow tree with green bark. I stepped in and the light went wrong."
2. "No day, no night. The star never moves, and under the leaves it might as well not exist."
3. "The caves glow and whisper sorry. I stopped counting."
4. "Don't say it. I did. The walls screamed."
5. "A pyramid that is a sphere. Latin on the wall: forgiveness, the Gardener, purple eyes turned green."
6. "The M.E.G. at Seedling will trade water. Their only way out is the tree in the middle of camp."

**Handbook outline**: what the Garden is; surface forest and canopy; glow rooms and Sorries; ruins and the plaque; the sentries and the phrase; the Heart of Water and Green Giants; Base Seedling; entrances/exits; creatures; advice (water, light, quiet).

## 3. Open questions
1. Fandom's Level 23 is Vienna: recommend ignoring it entirely.
2. Scale: lore is planet-wide; recommend the crust-patch plus core-basin reading above.
3. Surface light: lore says near-dark under the canopy yet "bathed in sunlight". Recommend dim green on the surface, colour in the glow rooms.
4. Entities not in the mod (Volpe, Curabitur Bird, Wrangler, natives): recommend Sorries as light props only, others later.
5. Sentry phrase needs a chat hook: optional, low priority.
6. Food: lore only says Base Seedling has limited food; fungus forage is a suggestion.
