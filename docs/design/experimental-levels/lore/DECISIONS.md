# Detail pass: the user's decisions (2026-10-30)

These override the briefs' open questions where they differ.

## Scope
- "World + content, unlinked": bring each level to the quality of the released levels (lore-faithful architecture,
  models, textures, props, light, sound bed) AND its content (the lore's entities, loot, wanderer notes, a handbook page).
- Still reached only through the cheats menu and `/backrooms goto`: no thresholds into or out of other levels, and
  Levels 0-20 must stay byte-identical (`layouthash`). Notes and the handbook may *mention* the lore's entrances and exits.
- When in doubt, follow the lore. Mark anything that is your own invention as such in the level's plan doc.

## New entities
- Build the ONE key creature or person each level's lore centres on: Level 73's **Coran** (blind chained guardian:
  never hostile to players, kills hostiles outside the castle), Level 120's **Misglitch** (spawns where a player dies in the
  level), Level 31's **Coach** (a friendly talking Faceling variant), Level 36's **Travel Planner** (a wanderer-style NPC).
- Everything else reuses existing mod creatures (Smiler, Hound, Faceling, Death Rat, Deathmoth, Skin-Stealer, Clump,
  Duller, Wretch, Roboman, corpse...). Minor lore creatures (Camo Crawlers, Sub-Misglitches, Volpe, Curabitur Birds,
  Wranglers, natives, Windows) are left for later; say so in the handbook where the lore names them.
