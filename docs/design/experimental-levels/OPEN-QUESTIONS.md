# Open questions for the user: DECIDED (2026-11-14)

The round-4 "Questions for the user" lists of the ten plans, plus the balance table's P-items and the 2026-11 review's
design leftovers, were consolidated and answered by the user on 2026-11-14 (research against the lore briefs, the plans,
`lore/DECISIONS.md` and the code; rule applied: when in doubt, follow the lore; mark inventions as such). "Keep" means
the build as of 0.700.0-pre.13 stands. The "To do" table at the end lists the work the decisions create; nothing blocks
the 0.700 release except where marked.

## Level 73 "The Redlands" ([73-plan.md](73-plan.md))
- Coran: no lantern, no hood. He is blind and armless and the level forbids light outside the castle; the tattered mantle stays (a marked invention).
- The dry ditch before the gate: **one block deep** everywhere (was two with one-deep ends). It is an invention, and the run must stay one a fast player can make.
- Beds: keep the shared bed under the castle canopy; no castle bed frame.
- Kitchen, pantry, cellar and solar: keep (marked inventions; they serve the Hound-meat fuel line). The visitors' book is done.
- Far fields: scene brightness **a shade up** from 0.44 so the far fields and random arrivals read (the lore's full moon).

## The Basement ([basement-plan.md](basement-plan.md))
- COLD BEER home bar: **about 25-30 % of rec rooms** (was 70 %); "barely any light sources save for the occasional light bulb".
- The Basement's own washer, dryer, sofa, TV and boiler replace the shared ones in Level 4's break rooms and Level 11's flats **after 0.700**, as in-place shape edits (block codes stay).
- Laptop on the bed in Flood dead ends: keep (the Wikidot account's "laptop ditched").
- Bare-stud hallways 45 %: keep. The Cincinnati plate and the January 1951 calendar: keep.
- DO NOT ENTER: on **about one swollen door in three**, not every one, so it stays a warning.
- P3, a surer way out: **one crack in three gets a ladder or rope that reaches the top**, and one wanderer note points at it; the other cracks keep the lore's "near impossible". Layout change behind version 16.

## Level 52 "School Halls" ([52-plan.md](52-plan.md))
- Clouds stay off until the cloud layer's seam with the sky sphere is solved (client work).
- Arrivals stay on paths and ground-floor corridors ("land in the same building or one nearby").
- Libraries stay nearly bare ("generally vacant and missing contents").
- No names over doors (the lore has four-digit numbers and no campus name anywhere; the M.E.G. gate boards carry the names). Keep the clock tower, lecture theatres, pitch, car parks and bus shelters. Lifts stay closed.

## Level 31 "Roller Rink" ([31-plan.md](31-plan.md))
- Barrier stays one block high ("a short wooden gate with flaps").
- Back arcade: **one room per complex** (was half the store rooms); the lore's "a back arcade area" is singular. The other store rooms go back to the repair-bench look.
- Litter stays light ("recently cleaned").
- STARDUST ROLLER RINK: keep (marked invention). No projector (the lore's cinema has a TV). Restocking drink fridges: keep (Class 0 is meant to be generous; the server is throttled).
- P1: the server's 12 game hours stand. P7: the revolving doors stay **unsigned** and keep their targets (a third to Level 9, as the lore names 9, 10 and 11); the surprise is the lore's.

## Level 120 "Broken Servos" ([120-plan.md](120-plan.md))
- Racks: **more doorless racks** (more to see by torchlight); the rest keep their perforated doors.
- Keep the level's own galvanised duct.
- Keep the fire-suppression bank, tape libraries and monitor-arm desks (marked inventions, all salvage).
- Hall letters stay letters (no numbers). Pencilled notes stay as they are. Lit EXIT signs on the way back to the offices: **one door in four** (was one in two); "usually pitch-black".
- P4: the Misglitch cap of 3 stands.

## Level 36 "Airport" ([36-plan.md](36-plan.md))
- One space-frame ceiling through the airside (check-in and the junctions keep their coffers): keep.
- The gallery's upper level stays a sight behind dark glass.
- **Add the "MEETING PLACE n OF 47" placard** at the meeting-point marker, n derived from the Meeting Place's position hash (the lore's "at least 47 distinct positions ... all one singular place").
- Customs in the claim hall: keep (marked invention). Linked sinks stay the pier restrooms and lounge showers only; concourse unit basins stay decorative.
- P2: food x0.35 stands (the user's 2026-11-13 call).

## Level 15 "Futuristic Halls" ([15-plan.md](15-plan.md))
- Robo-Man: **rebuild the one shared model** (joints, plates, emissive eyes, a wind-up key) for Level 15 and Level 73's castle alike; no second creature.
- Bulkheads stay every 32 blocks.
- Keep the battery lamp and the DO NOT RESET breaker at the edge of each dark square (lore silent on the transition; it reads as a power fault).
- Medical bays stay sleepable; hydroponics bays, robot docks and the "DO NOT ENTER THE STAR ROOM" slide stay.

## Level 23 "The Petrified Garden" ([23-plan.md](23-plan.md))
- Light shafts: **only over the glades and the Heart of Water** (was a scatter under the lid's holes); landmarks, and cheaper in the translucent pass.
- Architect statues at the ruins' ways in: keep (in the plaque's spirit).
- Bark relief stays nine blocks high until a frame-rate figure for a higher cut exists.
- P5 is applied: water Clean only at Base Seedling and the Heart of Water, Tainted elsewhere. Arrivals stay by the glades. The M.E.G. signs, notice board, trails and the lost explorer's camp stay.

## Level 14 "The Hospital" ([14-plan.md](14-plan.md))
- Keep the out-of-order lift and the directory board (set dressing, no mechanics).
- Keep 42 % dead troffers in the main halls ("shadowy hallways"); client-check the hall showcase shot once before ruling out a lighting fault.
- The Hunter: **a wet specular sheen on the skin and a faint glint at the tentacle mouths**, no emissive light (the lore's skin "absorbs all sources of light"); texture and shape edit.

## Level 47 "The Adderwood" ([47-plan.md](47-plan.md))
- Manor windows stay opaque old glass.
- Camp fires: **cold by default, alight in about one camp in four** (the camp cell stays one in sixteen); reads as the lore's "reverted" fires and never vanishes (the persistent map).
- The Great Adder: **damage scaled by the Danger setting, capped at 12-14 at the default** (was a flat 16); one strike still kills the unarmoured, armour and the setting count.
- Confirmed inventions: the vanilla moose for the aggressive deer, the Order summoning the Adder when a member is struck, the trail that only leads inward (the exit is a hollow tree).

## Cross-cutting
- Danger 0 for Levels 52 and 31 stays (Class 0, no cache bonus); a comment on the two rows says so.
- New entrances appearing in old worlds' unexplored chunks: accepted (2026-11-13); a line in DEVELOPING.md "Keeping saves working".
- Level 36 gate boards baked in pre-pre.11 tester worlds: accepted; the HUD and chat are authoritative; a changelog line.
- Container water class: single containers filled from a block carry the class since the 2026-11 review; poured water and bowls filled from a stack stay clean (documented in `docs/design/needs/decisions.md`).
- Spanish handbook index pages: **retranslate** the level and ways pages plus the marker-bearing bridge keys from the current English, with a langcheck test that fails on a page key whose Spanish text lacks the discovery markers. Main has the same problem; the fix carries to the 0.700 merge.
- BALANCE.md gets rows for Levels 14 and 47 (class, danger, solitude, the Hunter, the Adder and the Shucks) from a measured pass.
- Infinite IKEA: not for this mod; the mod page's "added soon" line gets a reply.
