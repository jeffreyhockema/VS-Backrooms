## 1. What the lore says (every element, quoted)

**The place.** "a regular, well-equipped hospital comprising infinitely segmented areas that contain shadowy hallways and
unsettling rooms." "Once someone enters the level, they will hear the constant sound of distant footsteps and whispers,
which can cause disorientation and unceasing paranoia." "The air is reported to be heavy with the scent of antiseptic and
decay." Survival Difficulty **Class 4**: "Unsafe", "Unstable", "Dangerous Entities and Hazards".

**The colony.** "a colony used to live in Level 14 as various remnants of civilization can be found in the level ... the
most popular theory is that The Hunter ended up killing the inhabitants ... T.14.R.G. (The Level 14 Research Group). Its
main objective was to gather as much information as possible about Level 14." Colonies: "All members presumed deceased",
"a large team of researchers", "They had a stockpile of supplies". Transcript 1 lists the twenty members (Dwight M.
Williams, project leader; researchers, security guards, "Containment Chamber Contractor"s, "Chemical Sample Collector and
Container"s). Transcript 2, "EXPERIMENT #1: GONE WRONG": Lawrence R. Afton, 25, injected with "Compound 14"; within 30
minutes distress, then aggression, "His skin had turned pitch black, and octopus-like tentacles started to stretch out of
his body. His eyes became bloodshot ... a vacant, almost lifeless gaze"; "he seemingly could sense whether someone was
nearby"; "Only 4 out of the 20 team members escaped the level alive"; "The survival difficulty of Level 14 has been
increased by three." Signed Dwight M. Williams.

**Hazards.** "In rare cases, the old, rusty hospital equipment in Level 14 may suddenly spring to life, malfunctioning and
causing harm to anybody nearby. These include faulty machinery, flying scalpels, stethoscopes, and syringes." "Certain
floor tiles of Level 14 may collapse and give way unexpectedly, revealing hidden passageways or leading wanderers to plummet
to their deaths ... pay attention to the ground. If there are any signs of instability, the best thing to do would be to
run." "Some of the rooms in Level 14 are contaminated and filled with toxic, radioactive substances, causing
hallucinations, disorientation, fear, and even memory loss ... check through windows into the room ... Although you still
must keep in mind that windows can be entities in disguise."

**Reception rooms.** "barren, mostly empty segments in which all of Level 14's entrants will first arrive. Housing little
more than an oval reception desk, a handful of chairs, and a lamp ... emanate a sense of discomfort ... Bandages,
antibiotics, and painkillers are often found at the front desk, and crumpled magazines and brochures of unusual contents lie
on the seats, as well as musty soft drink bottles, some with stale contents inside. Located in the back is a large,
stained glass, revolving door, depicting nothing beyond, acting as a 'spawn point' of sorts. These compartments may never
be entered again once left."

**Surgery rooms.** "vary in ... size, amount of decay, abandonment, and age. More often than not, one will find nothing but
a tilted hospital bed and a surgical light. These rooms may be overrun by mold or vegetation, shelves full of old and
damaged surgery equipment, or sometimes, even little kid posters. Growing moss/plants can be found growing on old test
tubes containing any form of liquid, though uncommonly. Doors to these rooms will often be found closed, rarely locked,
though surgery rooms are much harder to find."

**Inpatient rooms.** "medical, intensive care, behavior, and maternity units ... hospital beds, desks, cabinets, and various
medical equipment, all a blinding white shade, apart from the sparse corrosion and moss. These segments are some of the
only areas containing (shattered) windows of sorts, which exhibit nothing but blackness on the other side, just like the
reception room entrance door. Equipment is situated as if while being used ... previous ... inhabitants of the level
abruptly disappeared."

**Storages.** "nothing particularly significant ... apart from their architecture ... the walls appear to be asymmetrical
and appear almost thrown together ... the only notable thing inside these rooms is the extra supplies."

**Torture Chambers.** "a corroded, threatening atmosphere, with rusted stretchers stacked atop each other, a dark substance
confirmed to be blood dripping down the walls and ceiling, and a lining of a shattered marble floor. Metallic cabinets
containing an eerie quantity of stainless knives and razor blades haphazardly line the walls. These chambers are theorized
to be employed by The Hunter ... Unconfirmed reports ... drag those who wander too close to his grasp onto the beds ...
Torture Chambers indicate that The Hunter is drawing near — avoid them at all costs."

**The Hunter** (File 14-E). "also known as 'Patient Zero' ... one of the only entities that resides in Level 14, and its
main objective is to hunt down any wanderers." "skin is moist and completely pitch black, shrouded in some sort of darkness
that seems to absorb all sources of light. Its form is amorphous, with numerous writhing, octopus-like tentacles that
extend in all directions ... capable of fast and precise movements." "On each of The Hunter's tentacles, there are small
openings that resemble mouths with sharp, dangerous teeth. From the openings, The Hunter can release small leech-like
entities ... 'Infecting Agents' ... small, worm-like creatures that squirm along the ground extremely swiftly ... rows of
sharp, razor teeth ... spit out radioactive substances ... corrosive acids to life-ending toxins." "When The Hunter is
nearby, wanderers will suddenly feel a sense of dread. The temperature will drop drastically, and the air will become heavy
with a thick, smoky aura." "If you sense The Hunter is nearby, get out of the room immediately and run. Once you make eye
contact with The Hunter, there is no hope of saving you."

**Entrances.** A rare emergency exit door in Level 0; from Level 13 (unknown conditions); staircases on Level 130; the
windows of a hospital-like structure on Level 39; rotting drywall in Level 0.01. (Fandom 15 adds: "walking into a futuristic
door labeled 'Medical Center' will send you to Level 14".) **Exits.** A bright glowing window (777); arrows on the floor to
the main hospital exit (back to 130); "a rare futuristic-looking door will lead one to Level 15"; too far in (16);
toying with equipment (268); hugging a stuffed teddy bear (14.1); "a rare red exit door ... Level !"; harming oneself in a
torture chamber (4.4). (Wikidot 73 names Level 14 as a way into the Redlands, with no detail.)

## 5. The Hunter (design; our reading of File 14-E)

`src/Regions/L14Hunter.cs`. One Hunter per player in the level (3 at most), put down out of sight 36-60 blocks off (near a
torture chamber if one is within 96) after 150-300 s in the level, again 20-30 minutes after a kill. It "senses" its player: a
point near them re-picked every 20 s (error 30% of the distance), roaming slowly; within 24 blocks it stalks; seeing them
within 20, or **eye contact** (the player looking at it within 10 degrees and 32 blocks), it shrieks and charges (a 5 s burst a
little faster than a sprint, then sprint speed), lashes for about 7 at tier 3 and pulls you in ("drag those who wander too
close"). While chasing it lets loose 1-2 Infecting Agents every 10-15 s (4 at most): fast worms, 3.5 health, a venomous bite
(antibiotics clear it) and an acid spit you can sidestep; they die off after 40 s. Health about 91. Lost after 20 s unseen and
32 blocks. The client's atmosphere thickens to a cold smoky blue-black within 30-40 blocks of it, with a heartbeat. Not on the
director's roster (it is the level's only hunter, as the lore says).
