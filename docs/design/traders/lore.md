# Trader lore for the Backrooms mod's wanderers

Research notes, 2026-10-25. Nothing in the repo was changed. Raw page text is in `...\research\traders\raw\` (scratchpad).

Source tags used below:
- **WD** = Wikidot, https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/ (primary, and the mod was built from it)
- **FD** = Fandom API, https://backrooms.fandom.com/ (second opinion; Miraheze was not needed)
- Quotes are short. "Paraphrase" means it is not verbatim.

Gaps, stated up front:
- **"Children of the Backrooms"**: no page or group by that name on Wikidot or Fandom, and a web search found nothing. The only nearby canon is **"backborn"** (people born in the Backrooms; WD base-alpha, level-11). Treat any "Children" idea as invented.
- **Partygoers as traders**: not canon. They are a hostile cult (FD "Sanguine Festivus"); WD Level 601 only says they "frequent" The End and have taken wanderers. Do not make them traders.
- **Wikidot has no Level 3999 page** (404). Level 3999 comes from FD and the 2021 archive wiki (https://backrooms-wiki-2021-archival.fandom.com/wiki/Level_3999), as the mod's own `docs/design/new-levels/3999-plan.md` already notes.
- **Eyes of Argos**: canon does not say they trade. They are a law/justice order. Their settlement "The Enclave" is an Eyes commune with farmers/craftsmen/doctors, and Level 1's Cornucopia used to "provid[e] resources to the other settlements of the Eyes of Argos". I found no Eyes barter customs.

## 1. Trade and economy in canon

### 1.1 Currency, in one paragraph

There is **no universal currency**. Almond Water is the de facto unit of account.

- WD Object 1 (https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/object-1): "Since a standardized currency across multiple levels is generally impossible, many wanderers resort to bartering". Almond Water is "generally the most popular item used in bartering", "most commonly traded in places such as Level 5.1 and The Trader's Vault", and "used similarly to a single standardized unit of currency, such as 1 dollar, euro, or pound". The page advises carrying "a sizable quantity" on "heavily settled levels such as Level 11 or Level 6.1".
- WD Level 1 (https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-1), Tom's Diner: "Most Backrooms groups use bartering as a money system... A meal will cost you different resources depending on how elaborate it is." Bringing your own ingredients "reduce[s] the price".
- The **B.N.T.G. is the exception**: its own coin, the **"presses"** (WD level-11, https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-11; confirmed in WD the-b-n-t-g, footnote 3: "Trading here is mainly done via our own currency known as 'presses.'"). They are plastic coins; a focus-group quote says they are "essentially... Connect Four pieces". Outsiders passing through are exempted "as long as they trade at specific stores", and each such trade comes "complemented with a donation of presses... to familiarise people with presses".
- **Level 11's Capital** (non-aligned) has "a vague governmental system and currency-led economy" (WD level-11). Homes are free to employed residents ("two months' leeway for new inhabitants or those between jobs"); basic food and clothes are free supplies; luxuries ("anomalous objects, rare foods, building supplies, intricate clothing, and imports") cost money. The currency is not named.
- **Level 5.1** (casino) uses chips by colour: red $1, blue $5, yellow $10, green $20 (WD level-5-1, https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-5-1). Its barter table gives the only hard price list in canon (see 1.3). It is a trap level and not a model for a friendly trader.

### 1.3 Prices and scarcity (the only numbers in canon)

- Level 5.1, chip values for goods (WD level-5-1): Almond Water **2 red chips/bottle**; Deuclidators 1 blue/box; Memory Jars 4 red; Activated Charcoal 3 yellow/gram; Scarabacks 6 red; **Royal Rations 1 green per 1/4 lb**; Babel Balm 4 yellow/capsule; Maiden's Ink 4 green/bottle. In the log: "5 bottles of Almond Water and a half-pound of Firesalt" buys "a nice amount of chips", and a jackpot is worth "a whole bottle of Liquid Silence" ("how rare that crap is"). Read: Almond Water is cheap; Firesalt, Royal Rations, Liquid Silence are valuable. Note: in this entry's log, the trade is deliberately a rip-off.
- Level 8 supplies (WD level-8, footnote 5): Vitamin C "supplied by dried fruit and vegetables from B.N.T.G. trade caravans"; Vitamin D by "artificial UV light or vitamin pills sourced from Level 480". Caravans therefore stock **dried fruit, vegetables, vitamin pills**.
- Level 11 Capital: scarcity is mild; basics are free, extras cost (above).
- Level 1: resources come only from **crates** ("your only hope at survival"); crates "can disappear once not looked at" (WD level-1). Hence a trader's stock is whatever he found in crates.

### 1.4 Each of our sites: who is canonically there, what they have and want

**Level 1 camp.** Canon: "hundreds of small to medium encampments" of "a dozen of wanderers at most", in tents or huts, many unaffiliated (WD level-1). Likely a B.N.T.G. or Nomad-style camp near the Gild Sector, or a small independent camp. Has: crate goods (Almond Water, cans, clothes, bandages, matches, flashlights, yarn), landmarks with notes. Wants: crate finds, Firesalt, useful tools; "valuable items" for bartering. Warns: "Level 1 is so big... this place remains mostly rule-free."

**Level 4 outpost.** Canon: Level 4 is the level where Almond Water "can be found most frequently", with "water coolers, vending machines, and fountains containing Almond Water" and "lots of wanderers". It is "the best place to meet other people and find supplies"; M.E.G. Base Omega sits by the Level 5 and 6 entrances; Amor Incrementum and T.B.D. are the only named locals (WD level-4). Best fit: an **M.E.G. or independent outpost** that stocks up wanderers heading to Levels 5 and 6 ("stock up on Almond Water; it will be needed"). Note the page carries "being rewritten" and has thin text. Has: Almond Water in volume (cheap here), warnings about windows (blacked-out windows are fine; unblacked "are considered traps"). Wants: things the heading-deeper crowd carries back; oddities for T.B.D.-style collectors.

**Level 8, the 9th Road.** Canon: the Road is "marked out" by M.E.G. navigators with markers every ~50 m. Settlements "run by our very own operatives, in conjunction with our friends at the B.N.T.G. and the Harmouth Society. You can stop for a shower, a change of clothes, a hot meal, and a place to rest. If you've got things to trade, you can even hire a guide!" (WD level-8, M.E.G. Survival Guide Tip #1). Named: M.E.G. Outpost "Hollow Nest" (20 staff + ~24 rotating operatives), Harmouth camps, B.N.T.G. caravans. Has: hot food, caving suits, dried produce and vitamin pills (B.N.T.G.), guide service, markers. Wants: things to trade for guide service; fresh maps; warm clothes. Level fact: flashlights run at "12 lumens" in Level 8 (footnote 1); water is Almond Water (bitter, safe); baths are "a REALLY BAD IDEA".

**Level 11 corner.** Canon candidates: **B.N.T.G. New Times Square** (walled, "market stalls and shops", presses), the **M.E.G.'s Base Beta**, the non-aligned **Capital** market, the **U.B.D.S.** or **Malt Marters** nomads (WD level-11). A "corner" suggests an independent stall or a B.N.T.G. street vendor. Has: "anomalous items and necessary living materials", brochures and tapes ("tapes played to trigger entrance into [Level 201]... can be found or bought on Level 11"; "brochures to Level 222... are traded and bought across the Backrooms"). Wants: presses, Almond Water, rare Backrooms items. Warns: the Level 11 effect, barricaded streets, spray-painted trails ("not always accurate").

**The End, the Junkies.** Canon, verbatim: "A small group of 6 people have made an outpost in the fake realities of The End. They are not open for trading unless you have any amount of Memory Juice. The group spends most of their time intoxicating themselves on Memory Juice in an attempt to make themselves believe that they are home. The B.N.T.G. have made several attempts to rescue them but to no avail." (WD level-601, https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-601). The wiki filename is Level 601; the mod calls it The End. Memory Juice is mod-added (see end-plan.md), not an official wiki object I found.

**The Hub's outposts.** Canon: "Many different outposts... for various purposes, such as providing makeshift homes or trading posts." Two named: **M.E.G. Outpost "Doorstep Carpet"** (stores Level Keys "before they disappear"; "Any Wanderer can ask if a Level Key is available") and **Camp Amber Hub Outpost** ("limited supplies for any Wanderer"; "teach people what Level they are about to walk into"; "doesn't really have a fixed point") (WD the-hub, https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/the-hub). The B.N.T.G. routes goods through the Hub and the Vault sits "near to an entrance of the Hub" (WD traders-vault). So Hub traders = Doorstep Carpet staff, Camp Amber teachers, B.N.T.G. couriers. Level doors need keys; levels 1, 11 and 178 are always unlocked. The Keymaster ("antisocial") provides keys.

**Level 3999 camps.** Canon (archive/FD only): Singularity Point ("helps travellers to the exit, sells Almond Water"), Backroom Colonists ("cafés, trading"), Oasis Cara (guides, ~9,000 people). Level is safe and habitable; "all food is safe to eat". Exit: "Walk north till you find a glass door", then a ten-item checklist from Level 0 (https://backrooms-wiki-2021-archival.fandom.com/wiki/Level_3999). Has: Almond Water, café food, guides. Wants: little (it is a safe terminus); company and news. Attitude: kind, tired, near-finished.

## 2. Voice

### 2.1 Register by source

- **M.E.G. materials**: breezy, corporate-friendly, exclamation marks, capitalised emphasis, all-caps warnings, mascot cheerfulness. "Fret not — this guide is here to help!... (Probably. No guarantee.)" (Level 8). Sign-offs "M.E.G. — Bettering Humanity".
- **Wiki "second person" survival prose**: "Congratulations, wanderer!" "If you're reading this article, this means you have successfully escaped The Yellow Halls" (Level 1). Direct, reassuring, then blunt.
- **Survivor logs and notes**: weary, dry, short sentences. "I had simply gone deeper into the rabbit hole." "I never knew how much I could appreciate silence." (Level 1 vignettes).
- **B.N.T.G.**: corporate-brochure, polite, capitalist ("Peace through Prosperity", "Employment Opportunities", "Contact us"). Trader's Keep guide: "we at the Trader's Keep can and will keep you safe from the wilderness if you can pay for our protection."
- **Tom (Level 1 diner)**: warm, gruff, big-brother: "Kid, you think you're the first person to wander in here and have an existential crisis?" (Level 1).
- **Archivist/Frontrooms page**: gentle, haunted: "Please, don't end up like I have." (the-frontrooms)
- **Casino staff**: smooth, slightly sinister: "Anything and everything you could ever want, it's all here." (Level 5.1; never use for friendly traders)

## 3. Advice and rumours (one line each, with source)

**Almond Water (colours)** (WD object-1)
- Grey: most common, highest nutrition, no extra effect; "can temporarily tame... Deathmoths and Jerry".
- Green: sweetest, most caffeine; abuse causes insomnia and "an effect close to addiction".
- Red: bitter; cures colds and "the only known way to treat The Disease"; leaves you very tired.
- Blue: no aftertaste, melatonin up, sleepy; "interrupt[s] the Wretched Cycle" early; long abuse causes reliance.
- One bottle = a ~600 kcal meal; three a day suffices.
- A bottle not full to the brim may have been tampered with or carry the Crawler Fungus; "do not consume".
- Boil it with a pinch of Firesalt; too much makes it "slightly spicy".
- Some levels turn it cyanide-like; the level page says so.
- It was "the first object ever discovered by the M.E.G."

**Smilers** (WD entity-3): "attracted to light"; keep eye contact, walk away slowly, keep quiet, switch off or throw a light away; they sit in dark areas of Levels 2 and 3; panicked retreat or loud noise triggers an attack.

**Hounds** (WD entity-8): hostile on sight; "intimidated by direct eye contact"; don't run at once; "stay out of sight" if you hear growling; don't try to tame them.

**Skin-Stealers** (WD entity-10): wear skin; "Check anyone you are traveling with for clear blood" (their blood is translucent); "Don't trust people that barely speak, or say random assortments of words"; found mostly in Levels 0-2. Level 1 note: "Don't approach single humanoid presence when you're not sure they're humans."

**Facelings** (WD entity-9, level-11, level-3): not hostile as a rule; "only a few alleged reports of adult facelings retaliating in self-defense"; Level 11 ones are docile and the most numerous entity there; Level 3 ones are "hostile and aggressive". Ancient Lost texts called them "ignorant and underdeveloped", "powerless to cause any harm". Mod ties: they point the way.

**Deathmoths** (WD entity-4): attracted by bright light, so dim lights; avoid hives and clusters of female moths; males are docile scavengers; praetorian moths are the danger; on Level 2 they are smaller and docile.

**Dullers** (WD entity-6, level-1): stay away from walls on the "grating or rustling sounds"; "Run to it if you can, it will run away"; Almond Water on yourself or against them.

**Wretches** (WD entity-15, level-8): "the Wretched Cycle" comes from hunger, thirst and lack of sleep, isolation speeds it; stage 1 is itching and reversible with food, water, sleep; wash wounds with alcohol or "copious amounts of Almond water"; Blue/Red Almond Water helps; don't interact "even if you can understand their voice".

**Sleeping safely** (WD level-1): corridor rooms are "ideal spots to take a rest" (comfortable, enclosed); "Barricading the door with furniture can usually nullify a random intrusion"; avoid noise during Flickering; a sleeping bag is best, otherwise carpet or a mattress.

**Water safety**
- Level 1: ceiling-pipe puddles "aren't safe for consumption" (WD level-1, Aquila Sector); "do not drink or touch unsealed liquids" (Crawlers; WD level-1 table).
- Level 0: carpet fluids include "Almond Water, Liquid Pain, formalin"; "the Almond Water is tainted" (WD level-0).
- Level 8: Almond Water pools "generally safe to drink" but bitter; Level 8 streams of ordinary water are safe and "free of the sickening almond flavour" (WD level-8).
- Level 10: pit water is "deemed safe for human consumption" (WD level-10).

**Finding exits** (WD level-0, level-1, level-8): Level 0 exit is a flickering wall; Level 1 is "navigated by landmarks"; Level 1 navigation means fixing "a particular landmark at a reasonable distance" and walking at it; Level 8: follow the 9th Road to Level 9 and Level 11 ("the Dark Highway"); the Hub: level doors need a key and are one-way; Level 11: many doors lead out (shop types map to levels).

**Going home / Frontrooms**: no entrance ("If there was an entrance here, I'm sure we'd all have used it by now"; WD the-frontrooms). 60-70% die in Level 0 (WD manila-room). Only canon route home is Level 3999's glass door and a 10-item List (archive wiki). A trader should say: don't count on it.

**M.E.G. survival rules** (WD level-8 guide, level-1): stay on marked roads; stay hydrated, warm, awake; don't disturb spider nests; "stop, sketch or photograph" strange glowing emblems and report them; do not rush; carry a notepad and write who you are.

**Level 0 hazards** (WD level-0): "don't eat the carpets"; the Peripheral Shift rearranges halls; blackout zones; red rooms "must be avoided entirely".

**Level 1 crates** (WD level-1): can hold glass, nitrogen, chemicals; wear gloves; take only essentials; priority list below.

**Level 1 landmarks**: M.E.G. leave "a colorful piece of cloth with a group logo on it, with food or Almond Water and a note tied with it".

**Level 1 human danger**: "our species can easily be more monstrous than entities in a place with scarce resources."

**Level 8 flashlights**: a 100-lumen flashlight gives 12 lumens there (WD level-8 footnote 1).

**Cashew Water** (FD Object 44, https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Object_44): looks like Almond Water but is harmful; "affix a label"; the M.E.G. warns never to drink it.

## 5. Recommendations

### 5.1 Currency

1. **Use Almond Water as the in-world unit, and keep vanilla gears as the mechanical coin.** Canon says Almond Water is "used similarly to a single standardized unit of currency". The engine pays in gears, so rename or reskin them in the UI and dialogue ("a bottle's worth"), and price goods in "bottles". Rule of thumb from Level 5.1: **1 Almond Water = 1 unit; food and tools 1-3; Firesalt 3-6; knives and lanterns 6-8; Royal-Ration-class luxuries 10+.**
2. **B.N.T.G. traders count in "presses"** (plastic coins). If a real coin is wanted, add a **press** item as a mod item and let only B.N.T.G. sites use it; others barter.
3. **Barter flavour**: each site names a preferred barter good in dialogue ("Bring me Firesalt and I'll talk" at the Keep; "Bring Memory Juice or don't bother" at the Junkies). Keep Almond Water cheap on Level 4 and in 3999, dearer in Level 8 and the Hub.
4. **Do not let any friendly trader buy or sell Liquid Pain**; at most they warn.

### 5.2 Trader identities (one line each)

- **Level 1 camp**: *Pell, crate-picker* (unaligned, Nomad-leaning). Sells crate goods (Almond Water, cans, bandages, rope, candles); buys Firesalt, tools, spare cloth. Wary, tired, kind to newcomers; warns about humans.
- **Level 4 outpost**: *Dacre, M.E.G. Base Omega quartermaster*. Sells Almond Water cheap, bandages, candles; buys oddities and notes. Cheerful official; "Stock up on water, you'll need it in 5 and 6."
- **Level 8, 9th Road**: *Hollow Nest guide, with a B.N.T.G. caravan hand beside*. Sells hot food, dried fruit, vitamin pills, caving gear, guide help; buys maps, warm cloth, Almond Water. Brisk M.E.G. voice; "Stay on the Road."
- **Level 11 corner**: *Marla, B.N.T.G. street vendor from New Times Square*. Sells anomalous trinkets, brochures, tapes, candy; buys presses, bottles, rare items. Salesy, friendly, always counting.
- **The End, Junkies**: *Wick, one of the six*. Sells and buys only against Memory Juice; wants more juice, offers old finds and "home" mementos. Dreamy, evasive, deflects questions about leaving.
- **The Hub, Doorstep Carpet**: *Keyholder, M.E.G.* offers a key lookup and some supplies; **Camp Amber teacher** trades little, gives free advice and a few supplies. Calm, bureaucratic, a bit worn.
- **The Hub, B.N.T.G. courier**: *Courier on the Vault route*. Sells bulk goods, buys anything portable. Polite, businesslike; "Peace through Prosperity".
- **Level 3999 camps**: *Singularity Point guide* and *Colonist cafe keeper*. Sell Almond Water, food; buy stories and tickets. Gentle, unhurried; tell you to walk north.

(All names above are placeholders; Ale, Tom, Stephanie, Arthur, Diana, Louie are canon NPCs and best left to the wiki. Use the roles.)

### 5.3 Ten dialogue-tree topics

1. **Greeting**: "Congratulations, wanderer" and who we are (M.E.G./B.N.T.G./independent). Canon: Level 1 intro, B.N.T.G. motto.
2. **What are you selling?** Trade screen plus barter norms; "Almond Water is as good as money here"; presses at B.N.T.G. posts. Canon: Object 1, level-11.
3. **Water and food**: Almond Water colours and uses, boil it, puddles are unsafe, Level 8 and 10 safe, Level 0 carpets. Canon: Object 1, levels 0, 1, 8, 10.
4. **Entities**: Smilers (lights off, eye contact), Hounds (stare them down), Duller (stay off walls), Skin-Stealer (check blood), Facelings (usually harmless), Deathmoths (dim lights), Wretches (cure with water, sleep, Blue). Canon: entity pages.
5. **Sleeping**: barricade doors, corridor rooms, Blue Almond Water, fatigue and the Wretched Cycle. Canon: level-1, Object 1, entity-15.
6. **Exits and doors**: markers, landmarks, the Hub's keys, noclipping, "exits disappear once you lose sight". Canon: levels 1, 4, 8, hub.
7. **Going home**: the Frontrooms; honest "I do not think so"; Level 3999 glass door and the ten-item List. Canon: the-frontrooms, 3999.
8. **The groups**: M.E.G. (rescue), B.N.T.G. (trade), Lost (reclusive, "avoid contact unless necessary"), Eyes of Argos (justice, unfriendly to criminals), Harmouth (cavers). Canon: group pages.
9. **Rumours of this place**: site-specific lore (Level 1 Flickering, Level 4 windows, Level 8 spiders/emblems and the Road, Level 11 barricades and trails, The End's silence and terminals, Hub's one-way doors and Keymaster, 3999's attendants). Canon: each level page.
10. **Farewell / small talk**: hope lines ("Do not give up hope"), the Dark Highway, news. A grim joke from each site. Canon: level-1 vignettes, Tom.

Optional 11th for the Junkies only: **"Got any juice?"** gating the whole tree on Memory Juice (the mod's plan already has this: `endjunkie-nojuice` / `endjunkie-greet`).
