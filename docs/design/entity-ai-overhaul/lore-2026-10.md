## 1. Hounds (https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-8)

Current, rewritten by 1000dumplings (original Palico22), rev 56, 30 Sep 2025. Short page.

- Description: "a human with long black hair growing on the head. They have sharp claws, and an extremely large mouth
  with sharp teeth… often unseen due to the amount of hair." On all fours; "a lot of power when they attack a target,
  despite appearing sickly skinny."
- "Hounds become hostile the moment they see a human."
- "made aggressive when they see someone in an aggressive state."
- "they become intimidated when you give direct eye contact and stare them down. This won't work for long however."
- "if you hear a growling noise, its safest to stay out of sight."
- Don't: "Run away from Hounds the second you see one"; "Attempt to tame them."
- Origin: "Hounds hatch from eggs that originate from Level 276."
- Level 1's table: "intimidate it by visual contact and noise." Level 3: Hounds ambush from hiding and **bark to
  coordinate a pack** ("barking faster… when they would make a wrong turn towards a dead end").
- Levels: entity page "Majority". Level pages 1, 2 ("a rather common occurrence", unchanged through the blackout), 3,
  4 (one of only two species), 5, 9, 11 ("the most numerous entities", with Facelings), 94 (night). Fandom: Level !.
- Killing yields: nothing on Wikidot. **Fandom (Entity 8):** a rabies-like "Hound Virus"; "their unique pelt is sought
  after… one of the only sources of leather in the entire Backrooms" (the only lore for Hound hide); positive
  phototaxis; hear up to 2 miles; use sticks and rocks as tools. Fandom Level !: "Contrary to Smilers, they love light."

## 2. Skin-Stealers (https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-10)

Current, rewritten by 1000dumplings, rev 61, 9 Mar 2025.

- "Tall, pale yellow humanoids with sunken white eyes." Skin with microscopic bumps "similar to the suckers of an
  octopus' tentacle"; blood "completely translucent."
- Docile unless hungry or aggravated: "will wander aimlessly if they do not need to feed." Hungry, they "seek out
  humans wandering alone and… tear them apart with their hands." They wear the victim's skin, keep it warm with
  pumped blood and nutrients, digest it after about 24 hours, then go docile.
- They mimic speech without understanding it, but rarely speak. "Blood runs red, they're not dead. Blood runs clear,
  don't get near."
- Counters: walk away calmly, check companions for clear blood, distrust people who "barely speak, or say random
  assortments of words," don't aggravate a docile one. No weakness listed. Level 1: "Try discussing with them, as they
  posess low intelligence."
- Levels: entity page: most commonly 1, 2 and 3. Level pages: 1; **2: gone** ("used to be an entity that resided on
  Level 2 prior to the blackout. However, they have since vacated the entire level"; the heat "was known to aggravate
  them, as well as melt the decaying skin"; in the two-year blackout "they were left practically without any food…
  most to leave or starve"; "do not appear to have returned, and now mostly populate a select handful of the levels
  that connect to Level 2"); 3 ("the language capacity of an A2"); 5; 9; 94 (night).
- Killing yields: nothing.

## 3. Facelings (https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-9)

Current second rewrite by Nikuchan, rev 39, 2 Jul 2025. Habitat "Cosmopolitan".

- Human-like, no facial features; malformed organs they "do not even seem to need": no food, sight without eyes.
  Brittle bones.
- Variants: Common (any hair, skin, gender, age); Polygonal (boxy, flat-faceted, from "inadvertent no-clipping");
  **Shadow** ("pitch-black, spectral bodies… levitating within darker regions," "drawn toward sources of light," easily
  agitated but weak, "easily distracted with flashlights or torches"); Memory (fewer than 20 reports; look like loved
  ones); Evolved (partial malformed faces, can breathe, may have vocal cords).
- Origin: the Backrooms "replicating humankind", analogous to "level generation"; generated as infants, children or
  adults.
- "Most facelings are essentially docile." They mimic jobs, routines, festivals, religion; form friendships, romances,
  families. "easily angered by even small disturbances," escalating to "outbursts" with attacks and smashing. Fiercely
  protective of each other, especially children: "exceptionally aggressive if they notice any intentional attempt to
  harm another one of their kind."
- **Children:** "the most aggressive and unstable during outbursts… immediately begin hitting and scratching."
  "Applying traditional techniques to calm human children appears effective." (Level 6.1 log: an agent calms a child
  with vending-machine snacks; she clings to his leg; he names her Alice.)
- Counters: "physically weaker than most human adults, angry facelings are relatively easy to evade." Don't provoke
  one when others are near.
- **The "Level 11 Effect"** (defined on the Level 11 page): "a witnessed yet not understood phenomenon which causes
  hostile entities to be less inclined to attack. It is not a fool proof abnormality… deliberately provoking entities
  can cause the passivity to falter, … some entities are not affected by it at all." Level 3 adds it "does seem to
  affect every entity that is transported there." Level 6.1's are "Facelings with 'The 11 Effect'."
- Levels: 11 and any urban or human-populated level, 116, 6.1. Level pages: 2 ("Incredibly intelligent," dwindling);
  **3 ("every single one of them has displayed hostile and aggressive behavior," all Evolved)**; 6.1 ("only natural
  Entity," Snack Rooms staff); 11 (most numerous); Fun (fed to a maw).
- Killing yields: none. Don't "Taunt, provoke or attack." They die naturally of organ failure.
- Fandom: malicious and non-malicious kinds that look identical; child Facelings "carry sharp objects" and flee.
- *Wikidot Entity 11 is "Bursters"*: acid-spraying canine humanoids, Level 3 only; Almond Water "causes severe burns";
  harden into a cocoon when hurt. To-rewrite.

## 4. Deathmoths (https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-4)

Current full rewrite by DrAkimoto, rev 6, 31 Jan 2026. IETS 3E, "Multilevel; Migratory." *Saturnia mortem*.

- **Nests:** semi-organic, "digested biological matter, plant fibers, and secreted resin," 60–100 cm thick over the
  architecture; columns of indigestible remains (bones, hardwood, metal scraps); resin stops rot; satellite nests.
  Warning signs: "fleshy protrusions covering doorways," clusters of females.
- **Eggs:** 12–700 per laying, 3–5 mm to 3–5 cm in a fleshy incubation pouch, stacked in columns at the hive centre.
- **Larvae:** 5–10 cm, with **7 cm hollow quills that secrete a paralytic toxin**, serrated mandibles, a dozen legs;
  about 3 weeks eating the hive's upper layer, curl into a ball when threatened, then burrow into the wall for about
  20 days.
- **Common caste (~80%).** Males: wingspan 30–35 cm, needle proboscis, docile scavengers that drink "any liquid…
  including Almond Water and other supplies"; "a swarm can desiccate an incapacitated human in one to two hours"; scout
  far from the hive; most easily domesticated. Females: wingspan 40–45 cm, mandibles, a caustic-fluid organ;
  aggressive, more hostile nearer the hive; "hunt using swarm tactics… strip flesh from exposed areas," melt prey to
  "a gelatinous mass… shaped into spheres and carried back to the hive"; rally pheromones under stress.
- **Praetorian (~15%, at least one per colony):** "nearly a meter tall," eight 70–75 cm legs secreting potent
  paralytic toxin, semi-vestigial wings spreading pheromones up to 5 km, hermaphrodite, spits corrosive fluid;
  dismembers prey and hauls it home. "avoid Praetorian Deathmoths whenever possible."
- **Empress:** ~75 cm tall, 1.5 m body, nearly 3 m wingspan, short flights, pheromone range 15–25 km, escorted by
  Praetorians, lays the Praetorian eggs, "can cause a frenzied state"; one per level; migrates and seeds colonies. 13
  known migratory colonies.
- **Light:** "Bright lights can attract Deathmoths… extinguish or dim your lights." Nest sites have "limited access
  points and multiple light sources." Level 2: "more naturally attracted to light."
- Counters: S.W.A.R.M.S. (Survival over Engagement, Watch for Hives, Avoid Bright Lights, Remain Calm, Mark
  Territory, Stay Informed); a "Deathmoth Repellent" can; Level 3's are "completely immune to Deathmoth repellent" and
  go for the mouth.
- Levels: the entity page's body names none (backlinks: 0, 24, 77, 79, 80, 102, 103, 108, 116, 117, 406, 701…).
  Level pages: 2 (declining: left in the blackout or eaten by Smilers; repopulating); 3 (males aggressive too; over 40%
  Praetorians; a hive with **3 Empresses**); **5 ("one of the many central hives… Most fly out of dark rooms… the
  Females seem to stay in their nests")**; 8 (Handyland, New Movile Cave: insects and moss); 9; 94 (males only, at
  night). Fandom Level !: overhead swarms.
- **Uses and harvest:** larval quills: "certain groups within The Lost would hunt Deathmoths and track migratory
  patterns in order to harvest larvae… drain the larvae before removing and storing the quills. Their use in
  handcrafted blowguns made them an effective and often lethal weapon… still utilized by some groups." Domesticated
  males find no-clip points for the M.E.G. and are kept as companions. Praetorian venom is a stronger larval venom.
  **No silk, no larvae as food.** Death Rats eat male Deathmoths (Entity 24).
- Fandom (Entity 4): females secrete "moth jelly" that makes males submissive; Level 5 holds the most jelly though
  Deathmoths are now "nearly nonexistent" there; "rather than being drawn to bright lights… these moths seem to have an
  affinity for the shadows" (**contradicts Wikidot**); per-level variants (Level 8 venomous claws).

## 5. Dullers (https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-6)

Current, short, rewritten by Stretchsterz, rev 52, 7 Aug 2026.

- "Tall, grey humanoid, frail skeletal structure, with no facial features," no ears, long arms "that can extend to
  very far distances"; wobbly stance, unnatural gait; "surprisingly strong," "run at extreme speeds, even while holding
  items twice their own weight."
- **Wall reach:** it "hunts by being in a hall, and having a victim in a hall on the other side of a wall. The Duller
  will then no-clip its arm through the wall capturing the prey… and then drag them into their hall."
- **Cowardice:** "They usually run away from anything that gets close… If you notice a Duller, without their hunting
  tactic, they will silently run away."
- **Theft:** "They can sometimes take seemingly random objects, and it is unknown what they do with the items."
- **Almond Water:** "seem to dislike Almond Water… avoid Almond Water fountains, and other items."
- "It is unknown what they do to you once captured."
- Counters: "Specifically run to it if you can, it will run away." "Use Almond Water on yourself, or against them."
  Don't "Lose your Almond Water"; don't stand "on the opposite wall from the Duller." Level 1: "grating or rustling
  sounds" from walls.
- Levels: "Majority," "generally found in the lower levels." Level pages: **1 and 4** (with Hounds, the only species
  in 4).
- Killing yields: nothing. Fandom (Entity 6): intelligent psychological torturers with hallucination "trances"; no
  cowardice.

## 6. Death Rats (https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-24)

Old anonymous page with an "This article is being rewritten!" banner, rev 25, 1 Jun 2025.

- "a normal house rat, with a long body, and black fur. They also have **strange brown horns coming out of their head,
  that has a red glow on the tips**." Populations differ per level; Level 2's are hairless.
- They "evolve their bodies and abilities to adapt to various Levels."
- **Screaming Event** (Level 1): "you can hear screaming… from Rats chasing other Entities. Lights will flicker during
  this event, until the Death Rat stops or the Entity is terminated."
- Eat "Male Deathmoths and Mold"; can kill other entities. "some species are very dangerous and will even set traps for
  you. They are smarter than you think." The Lost: "The Forever Changing Rodents."
- Counters: none; avoid, don't aggravate.
- **Killing yields:** "most Death Rats are harmless and **can be a food source if your willing to eat one. Somehow.**"
- Levels: 1; 2 ("virtually zero threat… feed on the liquids in barrels, or on abandoned foodstuffs"; "The corpses of
  Death Rats are… more frequent than the sight of a Death Rat themselves": killed, suffocated or boiled in machinery;
  bald from the heat); **3 ("engineer rats": capybara-sized, sharpen teeth on the bars, traps "always involve
  primitive electrical equipment… bombs, drones, and gas grenades", lead the attacks on Base Gamma, "human-level
  intelligence… read and write in English… text-to-speech devices")**; 5 ("Deathrats"); 8 (Rottnest Jungle ceiling);
  9; 94 ("evolved to look like normal rats").

## 7. Clumps (https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-5)

"This article is outdated!", to-rewrite, rev 38, 23 Sep 2025. Two layered versions: Kekcroc's original, then
TrailmixNCocoa's in-character rewrite.

- "a bundle of human limbs," sometimes with eyes and ears. Original: up to ~3 ft, "incredibly dry skin," bones
  "extremely brittle." Rewrite: up to ~4 ft, skin "sticky, almost dewy," smells of "burnt flesh and red dahlias."
- Speed: swiping at the floor; "tremendous speed," "jumping over obstacles, traveling through vents, and climbing up
  walls."
- Grab: "Within eight feet, it will stretch out its longest limb… bringing them towards its center to reveal a mouth
  filled with razor-sharp teeth." Longest limb 8–10 ft.
- Hunger: "constant state of hunger"; the original's Overseer note: "deliberate starvation, ignoring almost every
  wanderer… unless provoked."
- **Dormancy (rewrite):** "Once they've eaten their fill, Clumps will enter a dormant state… dig into the ground (or…
  break through the carpet/concrete)… leaving only their longest limb out… It is best to exterminate them during this
  state."
- **Regeneration:** "when a Clump is harmed — its flesh splattered onto the ground and left uncleaned — a new, smaller
  Clump will grow from the ground… compared to the way a flower grows."
- Counters: silent, at least 8 ft away; "Retreat at the sound of multiple people running"; Wall Masks (Oni Masks)
  make them "bow or flee"; **Clumpshot** (Object 23, exploding shotgun pellets, "works well on wretches too"; Level 3:
  "The most reliable way to deal with clumps remains Clumpshots"); Level 3 Clumps beaten by "the chaos and
  disorganization between their arms."
- Levels: original "Majority, most notably Levels 2 and 3"; rewrite "Levels 2 and 10". Level pages: 1 ("rushing to
  their preys with high speed… they possess no eyes"); 2 ("most common", "most lethal", ambush from corners and
  intersections); 3 (up to 8 ft, weapons and Squirt Guns, self-mutilate to expose bone, lift weights).
- Lore: "The Ones with Eternal Limbs"; made from willing humans by a banished creator; sister creation Snatcher Weeds.
- Fandom: ambush from vents, closets, pipes; dangle arms as bait; drum on walls; 20 mph; no regrowth.

## 8. The Beast (Gentleman) of Level 5 (https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-18)

Current, by ratscrapz, rev 54, 13 Aug 2026. See also `docs/design/entity-briefs/beast.md` on branch `l5-beast`.

- Two voices. The Beast as host: "This is my hotel. My domain… Why not the Gentleman of Level 5?… Let's strike a
  deal… You just have to do a few things for me, nothing too difficult… And I promise you'll never have to feel that
  fear ever again." A handshake ("A deal is a deal, right?"), then "Why are you doubting me?… I thought I made it clear
  not to doubt me." Habitat: "Right where I need to be."
- A deal-taker's warning: "he's going to watch you until he can see you break. Don't trust the portraits or the
  patterns in the wall… He can smell your fear… I'm not finishing my end of this dumb deal." "**He only makes himself
  seen to those he wants to be seen by.** If you spot that… thing out of the corner of your eye it's already too
  fucking late. You don't discover him. He discovers you."
- No physical form, no hostility shown; the threat is psychological and contractual. Counters: "Just get out of
  there." "don't listen to his voice, don't look at the portraits."
- Level 5 only; the page has "an unseen presence watching them around every corner… whispers… something tapping their
  shoulder… watching eyes from the aged paintings." Level 5's page has him speak: "Why don't you come to my office?"
- Fandom (Entity 18): physically present and hostile; "a chimaeric conglomeration of human, octopus and deathmoth
  dressed in a furred black suit"; camouflage; warps hotel architecture; soul-for-desire deals, "always keep[s] his

  instantly lose his cordial demeanour and begin to enact a physical attack"; thought to hold Level 5's key.

## 9. The Neighborhood Watch (https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-96)

Current, rewritten by Ruebekit, rev 70, 10 Oct 2026. Level 9 only: "they never intentionally cross into other
levels." The Lost: "The Ones That Gaze Into Souls."

- **Senses:** "keen sight and touch but lack hearing and smell. They don't require food." Don't: "Be afraid to make
  noise near them." Medium intelligence; they herd prey into one another.
- **Watchers:** "gigantic eyeball-like structures with multiple optic veins… levitate effortlessly at walking speeds
  and silently glide over the suburban areas." Beam: "a beam of light from their pupils that instantly turns any living
  matter fully absorbed in the light into fine, gray dust" (harmless to other Watch). No man-made weapon has destroyed
  one; don't engage "or use backrooms objects such as Firesalt to destroy them." No sound, no trace.
- **Striders:** "six 8-foot appendages composed of choroid, nerves, and veins" beneath one eye; "up to 90 mph"; reach
  into buildings ("hiding in shallow rooms unadvisable"), grab occupants and "pummel their target onto nearby
  surfaces." Ranged weapons work on them (gunfire and Firesalt draw other entities). Most vigilant; climb houses.
- **Swimmers:** dog-sized, "eight optic nerves arranged similarly to octopi"; squeeze through pipes and hoses; crawl
  out of water to chase, "submerge and drown" victims, or tear off limbs.
- **Where:** "dark corners when they're not patrolling… places with dead ends and lots of houses. You don't see them
  as much in open spaces or areas that go to Level 10." Swimmers: "sewers and all those drain systems… puddles on the
  streets." Striders: "common to see one on top of a house." Watchers: "floating in the streets or above the houses."
- **Electronics:** nearby devices glitch, distort, then die; fill with unknown images, audio and music. Don't "Power on
  electronics in Level 9."
- **Pockets (Object 51):** carrying one alerts them; a Strider or Swimmer takes it back.
- Dos: stay hidden, carry a weapon (Striders and Swimmers can be fended off), stay away from water, record in writing.
- **Effect on other entities:** "Deathmoths, Smilers, Skin-Stealers, and Hounds all seem to fear these creatures."
  They will enter the Mangled's fog.
- Killing yields: Watcher victims are gray dust "like salt or sugar" in piles beside phones, glasses and bottles. Dead
  Swimmers and Striders turn up (a Strider "all burned up and full of holes"). A log speculates on "using swimmers as a
  food source, maybe by cooking them."
- Fandom (Entity 196): same three types; they "cannot directly detect living organisms, but can locate targets by
  tracking signals emitted by electronic devices"; stop mid-chase to stare at old telephones; "the edges of the dark
  suburbs"; "pale gray ash."
