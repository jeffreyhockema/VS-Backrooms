# Vintage Story 1.22.7 creature AI: what vanilla offers and what the mod must build

Survey for the [entity AI overhaul](README.md), made 2026-10-18 from decompiled 1.22.7 DLLs (dated 2026-08-16, in
`G:\Games\Vintagestory`). Decompiles were found in earlier sessions' scratchpads (`…\e3c076f8-…\scratchpad\decomp\`
with `all_api`, `all_ess`, `all_surv`; `…\17fb4aec-…\scratchpad\decomp\LibFull\` for VintagestoryLib). Those folders
may be gone; decompile again with `ilspycmd` (run with `DOTNET_ROOT="/c/Program Files/dotnet"`). Below, `ess/` means
`all_ess/Vintagestory.GameContent/`.

**Two task systems run side by side in 1.22.** Legacy tasks (`AiTaskSeekEntity`, read through `taskConfig[...]`) are
what every vanilla entity JSON uses. "-R" tasks (`AiTaskSeekEntityR`, typed `[JsonProperty]` configs such as
`AiTaskSeekEntityConfig`) are registered as `seekentity-r`, `meleeattack-r` and so on (`ess/AiTaskRegistry.cs`). No
vanilla asset uses an `-r` code. The R system holds the perception options (light-scaled detection, sneak scaling,
herd filters).

## 1. Perception

**Line of sight: partial.** `AiTaskBaseTargetable.hasDirectContact(target, minDist, minVerDist)` and R's
`HasDirectContact` (`ess/AiTaskBaseTargetable.cs:403`, `ess/AiTaskBaseTargetableR.cs:410`) check distance, then cast up
to 3 `World.RayTraceForSelection` rays from feet+1/32 and about 7/16 of the body height. Only block **collision boxes**
block the ray (glass blocks sight; leaves without collision do not). The legacy third ray is a copy of the second.
Used only by melee, ranged (`AiTaskShootAtEntityR`, `AiTaskTurretMode(R)`, `AiTaskThrowAtEntity`) and the boss tasks.
**`AiTaskSeekEntity`, `AiTaskFleeEntity`, `AiTaskStayCloseToEntity` and `AiTaskLookAtEntity` do no sight check**:
seeking works through walls inside `seekingRange`. **No last-seen memory anywhere**: seek re-reads `targetEntity.Pos`.

**View cone: must be built.** Only aim/turn limits exist (`attackAngleRangeDeg` default 20, `maxTurnAngleDeg`,
`MaxThrowingAngleDeg`).

**Sneaking: exists.** Legacy `CanSensePlayer`: `eplr.Controls.Sneak && OnGround` multiplies range by a hard-coded 0.6.
R: `Config.SneakRangeReduction` (default 1) in `GetDetectionRangeMultiplier`. Both also multiply by the player stat
`animalSeekingRange` (R can switch that off with `SeekingRangeAffectedByPlayerStat`).

**Target light level: R only.** `AiTaskBaseTargetableConfig.TargetLightLevels` (int[4], default `[0,0,32,32]` =
ignored) and `TargetLightLevelType` (default `MaxTimeOfDayLight`); `GetTargetLightLevelRangeMultiplier` is a trapezoid
(0 at or below `[0]` and at or above `[3]`, 1 between `[1]` and `[2]`). It reads `BlockAccessor.GetLightLevel(target.Pos
.AsBlockPos, type)`: world light only; a held torch is not included (the mod's `Senses.LightAt` / `WithCarried` adds
it). The entity's own light: `EntityLightLevels` / `EntityLightLevelType` gate whether a task runs; `AiTaskWander` has
`preferredLightLevel`; legacy `AiTaskFleeEntity` has `minDayLight` / `ignoreDeepDayLight`.

**Hearing: only in the bell tasks.** `AiTaskBellAlarmR.CheckDetectionRange` (`ess/AiTaskBellAlarmR.cs:189`) reads
`ServerControls`: standing still, no clicks, on the ground gives `SilentSoundRangeReduction` (0.25); sneaking
`QuietSoundRangeReduction` (0.5); not listening `NotListeningRangeReduction` (0.5). A guess from controls, not sound
propagation. **Smell: not found.**

**Targetable entity.** Legacy `IsTargetableEntity` (line 277): `e.Alive`; `minTargetInjury`/`maxTargetInjury`;
`EntityTags` with weight; `entityCodes` (prefix `*`). Legacy `CanSense`: not itself; `TargetOnlyInteractableEntities`;
`skipEntityCodes`. Legacy `CanSensePlayer`: world config `creatureHostility` (`aggressive`/`passive`/`off`; with
`passive` the creature targets only in `aggressiveondamage` or `aggressivearoundentities`; `friendlyTarget` skips it),
the sneak rule, `animalSeekingRange`, same dimension, `targetablePlayerMode` (not creative/spectator, Playing). R adds
`IgnoreDeadEntities`, `TargetEntitiesWithSameHerdId`/`DifferentHerdId`, `MinTargetWeight`/`MaxTargetWeight`,
`TargetEverything`, `TargetPlayerInAllGameModes`, `TargetSearchCooldownMs` (2000), `SearchType`, `SeekingRange` (25),
`UseFearReductionFactor` with `TamingGenerations`, `RetaliateAttacks`, `TriggerEmotionState`, `CreatureHostility`.
**Low stability:** `lowStabilityAttracted` and `RequiredTemporalStability` (0.25) only in `AiTaskFleeEntity`; the
entity attribute `spawnCloserDuringLowStability` stops fleeing players at stability ≤ 0.25.

**Entity state tags: exists.** `EntityBehaviorEntityStateTags` (`entitystatetags`, on `player.json`) keeps
`state-sneaking`, `state-sprinting`, `state-moving`, `state-on-ground`, `state-swimming`, `state-flying`,
`state-feet-in-liquid`, `state-aiming`, `state-armed`, `state-armed-melee`, `state-armed-ranged`, `has-open-fire`. A
task's `entityTags` condition can target "sprinting players" with no code; tasks can tag their own entity while running
(`TagsAppliedToEntity`).

## 2. Emotion states (`ess/EntityBehaviorEmotionStates.cs`, `ess/EmotionState.cs`)

Exists. Per state: `code`, `duration`, `chance`, `slot`, `priority`, `stressLevel`, `maxGeneration`, `accumType`
(`Max`/`Sum`/`NoAccum`), `whenHealthRelBelow` (999), `whenSourceUntargetable`, `entityCodes` (for
`aggressivearoundentities`), `notifyRange` (12), `belowTempDuration`/`belowTempThreshold`. `NotifyEntityCodes` and
`NotifyChances` exist but nothing reads them.

Engine-triggered: on damage (unless fall/drown or hostility `off`): `alarmherdondamage` (also triggers
`aggressiveondamage` on same-`HerdId` agents within `notifyRange`), then `aggressiveondamage` (→
`aggressivealarmondamage`), then `fleeondamage` (→ `fleealarmondamage`); every 0.33 s `aggressivearoundentities`
(aggressive worlds); `saturated` after a melee kill; `fleeondamage` from `EntityShiver`; any state from a task's
`triggerEmotionState` or the activity system's `TriggerEmotionStateAction`.

`TryTriggerState(code, [rnd,] sourceEntityId)` rolls `chance`, then `tryActivateState`: fails if `whenHealthRelBelow <
healthRel`; another active state in the same `slot` with higher `priority` blocks it, otherwise the first with equal or
lower priority is evicted; then `maxGeneration`, then the duration by accum type. `whenSourceUntargetable` instead
pathfinds toward the source asynchronously and activates only if **no path** was found (the "can't reach you" state).
Durations lose `10 × dt` per 0.33 s tick (about seconds). Wolves: `fleeondamage` 60, `aggressiveondamage` 30,
`saturated` 1200. Active `StressLevel`s sum into WatchedAttribute `stressLevel`.

Quirks: `healthRel` only updates in `OnEntityReceiveDamage` and starts at 0, so every `whenHealthRelBelow` passes
before the first hit; eviction removes the *new* code's attribute (bug); `Sum` reads like a precedence bug.

Tasks gate with `whenInEmotionState`/`whenNotInEmotionState` (legacy, `|`-separated, obsolete) or
`WhenInEmotionStates`/`WhenNotInEmotionStates` (arrays), in `PreconditionsSatisfied`. Custom codes work;
`GetBehavior<EntityBehaviorEmotionStates>().TryTriggerState(...)` / `IsInEmotionState(...)` from any code.

## 3. Pathfinding and movement

**Pathfinder: exists.** `EntityBehaviorTaskAI.PathTraverser` is a `WaypointsTraverser`: `NavigateTo`,
`NavigateTo_Async`, `FleeFrom`, `Flee_Async`, `WalkTowards`, `FollowRoute`, `PreparePathfinderTask`, `Stop`, `Retarget`.
Async searches queue on the `PathfindingAsync` mod system; `searchDepth` defaults to 999. `StraightLineTraverser`
exists. `AStar.traversable` uses the entity's `CollisionBox`, `StepHeight` (from `controlledphysics`) and
`maxFallHeight` (`4 - movingSpeed*30` with fall damage on and no `reckless` attribute, else 12). Per-block cost:
`Block.GetTraversalCost(pos, EnumAICreatureType)` (`Default`, `LandCreature`, `Humanoid`, `HeatProofCreature`,
`SeaCreature`, set via `aiCreatureType`); above 10000 is impassable. **Mod blocks can override `GetTraversalCost`.**

**Climbing and swimming: exists.** `EntityProperties.CanClimb`, `CanClimbAnywhere`, `ClimbTouchDistance`;
`controlledphysics` takes `stepHeight`, `stepUpSpeed`, `climbUpSpeed`, `climbDownSpeed`, `climbAnywhereStepThreshold`.
Water from `Habitat` and the creature type.

**Doors: must be built.** No AI task opens doors; A* treats a closed door as solid. Hook: `BlockBehaviorDoor.Activate
(world, caller, blockSel, activationArgs{opened})` → `BEBehaviorDoor.ToggleDoorState(null, opened)`; vanilla calls it
only from the villager activity system.

**Through walls: must be built.** `EntityControls.NoClip` is honoured only by `EntityBehaviorPlayerPhysics`;
`EntityBehaviorControlledPhysics` always calls `ApplyTerrainCollision`; passive physics collides too. Options: subclass
`EntityBehaviorControlledPhysics` (virtual `SetModules()`, `OnPhysicsTick`, `HandleSteppingOnBlocks`) or move `Pos` by
hand, with a custom traverser since A* will not plan through blocks.

**Flying: partial.** No flying physics; butterflies and Erel use `controlledphysics` with `habitat: "Air"`, and
`PModuleGravity` skips gravity for `Air`, `Sea`, `Underwater`. Fly tasks: `flywander`, `flycircle`, `flycircleifentity`,
`flycircletarget`, `flyswoopattack`, `flydiveattack`, `flyleave`, `firefeathersattack`, the butterfly tasks.

## 4. Hearing and noise

**Must be built.** Server `PlaySoundAt` (`LibFull/Vintagestory.Server/ServerMain.cs:3080-3201`) only sends packets to
clients in range. No sound event, listener interface or entity callback (`OnEntitySound`, `EntityBehaviorHearing`,
`IEntityListener`, `EntityBehaviorNoise`: not found). `sound`, `soundStartMs`, `soundRepeatMs`, `soundRange`,
`soundChance`, `finishSound` on `AiTaskBase(Config)` only play the creature's own sound.

**Delivery channel: exists.** `Entity.Notify` → `EntityBehaviorTaskAI.Notify` → `AiTaskManager.Notify`
(`ess/AiTaskManager.cs:389`). Built-in keys `"starttask"`/`"stoptask"`; any other key goes to every task's
`Notify(key, data)`, and a task returning true is started if its priority beats the slot's `PriorityForCancel`.

A hearing system needs the mod's own noise bus: player inputs from `ServerControls` (`Sprint`, `Jump`, `TriesToMove`,
`LeftMouseDown`, `HandUse`), block events (break, place, door toggles) or Harmony on `PlaySoundAt`, delivered by
`Entity.Notify` or polled by the task.

## 5. Attacks and status effects

**Melee: exists.** Legacy `AiTaskMeleeAttack`: `damage` (2), `knockbackStrength` (default √(damage/4)),
`attackAngleRangeDeg` (20), `attackDurationMs` (1500), `damagePlayerAtMs` (1000), `minDist` (2), `minVerDist` (1),
`damageType`, `damageTier`; line of sight at hit time; `attackTarget()` virtual. R adds `MaxAttackDistance`,
`MaxAttackVerticalDistance`, `DamageWindowMs`, `TurnToTarget`, `EatAfterKill`, `PlayerIsMeal`, `IgnoreInvFrames`,
`AffectedByGlobalDamageMultiplier`, `RetaliateUnconditionally`. `animationSpeed` is a base field. Variants:
`meleeattacktargetingentity`, `jealousmeleeattack`, `eidolonmeleeattack`, `eidolonslam`.

**Ranged: exists.** `throwatentity` (`projectileCode`, `projectileDamage`, `maxDist`, `releaseAtMs`, `yawInaccuracy`),
`turretmode(-r)`, `shootatentity-r` (projectile code and item, damage, dispersion, speed, gravity, `ShootSound`).
Projectiles: `EntityProjectile`, `EntityProjectileBase`, `EntityThrownStone`, `EntityThrownItem`,
`EntityThrownSnowball`, `EntityThrownBeenade`. `stayinrange(-r)` keeps distance.

**Grab or hold: must be built.** `EntityBehaviorAttachable` is gear slots; `Seatable`, `Rideable`, `CreatureCarrier`
are mounting; no AI grabs. "DrifterRise" not found.

**Status effects: partial.** `DamageSource.Duration`, `TicksPerDuration`, `DamageOverTimeType` →
`EntityBehaviorHealth` `DamageOverTimeEffect`s (`ActiveDoTEffects`, `ApplyDoTEffect(...)`, `StopDoTEffect(type)`;
`EnumDamageOverTimeEffectType` `Unknown`/`Poison`/`Bleeding`; bandages stop bleeding). No melee config sets them:
override `attackTarget`/`AttackTarget` or call `ApplyDoTEffect`. `EntityBehaviorBleeding`/`Poisoned`: not found. Other
levers: `Entity.Stats.Set("walkspeed", …)`, `EntityBehaviorHealth.onDamaged`, `EntityBehaviorBodyTemperature`,
`EntityBehaviorTemporalStabilityAffected.OwnStability`.

## 6. Harvesting and drops

**Exists** (`ess/EntityBehaviorHarvestable.cs`). JSON: `drops` (`BlockDropItemStack[]`), `duration` (5 s; must match
client and server), `fixedweight`, `quantitySlots` (4). Quantity scales by WatchedAttribute `animalWeight`,
`deathByMultiplier` on the killer's type (0.4 when a non-player kills), crushed (0.5) or acid (0.25) deaths, the player
stat `animalLootDropRate` (skipped with attribute `isMechanical`), optional `DropModbyStat`. `BlockDropItemStack`:
`Type`, `Code`, `Quantity` (NatFloat), `Attributes`, `LastDrop`, `Tool` (an `EnumTool` filter, usable as a condition),
`DropModbyStat`. **Attribute-dependent drops: exists.** Any behaviour or class implementing
`IHarvestableDrops.GetHarvestableDrops(world, pos, byPlayer)` adds stacks (gathered via
`entity.GetInterfaces<IHarvestableDrops>()`). `{type}` placeholders resolve per variant. Death drops:
`EntityProperties.Drops`, replaceable by `EntityBehavior.GetDrops(..., ref handling)`. Not found:
`ByStackTypeDropCondition`, `dropOnlyOnKill`, `lastDroppedItemStack`. `EntityBehaviorDeadDecay`: `hoursToDecay` (96),
`decayedBlock`.

## 7. Sensing helpers (all exist, server side)

`EntityPartitioning` (mod system): `GetNearestEntity(pos, r, match[, EnumEntitySearchType])`,
`GetNearestInteractableEntity`, `WalkEntities`, `WalkInteractableEntities`, `WalkEntityPartitions`.
`BlockAccessor.GetLightLevel(pos, EnumLightLevelType)` (`OnlyBlockLight`, `OnlySunLight`, `MaxLight`,
`MaxTimeOfDayLight`, `TimeOfDaySunLight`, `Sunbrightness`). `EntityPos.AheadCopy`, `HorizontalAheadCopy`,
`GetViewVector()` (`ServerPos` is an obsolete alias of `Pos`). `GetBehavior<EntityBehaviorHealth>()`: `Health`,
`MaxHealth`, `ActiveDoTEffects`. Player inputs: `EntityAgent.ServerControls` is authoritative on the server (prefer it
over `Controls`, as `Senses.Running` does). Held light: `EntityPlayer.LightHsv` merges both hands (throws with a null
`Player`: keep `Senses.HeldLight`/`RightHand`); never in `GetLightLevel`. Herd ids:
`sapi.WorldManager.GetNextUniqueId()`; `EntityAgent.HerdId` settable.

## 8. Spawning and packs

`BaseSpawnConditions`: `Group`, `MinLightLevel`, `MaxLightLevel`, `LightLevelType`, `HerdSize`, `Companions`,
`InsideBlockCodes`, `RequireSolidGround`, `TryOnlySurface`, `ClimateValueMode`. `RuntimeSpawnConditions`: `Chance`,
`MaxQuantity` (20), `MaxQuantityByGroup`, `SpawnCapPlayerScaling`, `MinDistanceToPlayer` (18). `WorldGenSpawnConditions`:
`TriesPerChunk`. The spawner gives each group one `HerdId`. `EntityBehaviorDespawn`: `minPlayerDistance`,
`belowLightLevel`, `minSeconds`, `afterDays`.

**Packs: basic.** `EntityBehaviorHerd`/`AlarmHerd`: not found. Herds are `HerdId` plus two broadcasts: `AiTaskSeekEntity`
`alarmHerd: true` (R: `AlarmHerd` + `HerdAlarmRange`), which on acquiring a target sends `Notify("seekEntity", target)`
to same-herd agents in range; and the emotion state `alarmherdondamage`. Vanilla wolves use only the latter. Flanking,
encircling and roles must be built (`AiTaskSeekEntity` has an `OnCircleTargetUnable` fallback).

## 10. "Is the player looking at me?"

Reliable. Each client position packet carries `Yaw`, `Pitch`, `Roll`, `HeadYaw`, `HeadPitch`, `BodyYaw`; the server
writes them to `entity.Pos` (and `EntityAgent.BodyYawServer`) in `EntityPosExtensions.SetFromPacket`
(`LibFull/Vintagestory.Server.Systems/EntityPosExtensions.cs`, from `ServerUdpNetwork.cs:283/389`). So
`player.Pos.GetViewVector()` is the look direction, as often as packets arrive. This is what `Senses.AngleTo`/`LooksAt`/
`Sees` use (`src/Entities/Senses.cs:147-169`) with `ClearLine`. Third-person and free-look were not verified; treat the
stare test as first-person accurate.

## Summary

**Must build:** view cones, last-seen memory, sound propagation and hearing, smell, door opening (the mod has its own),
moving through walls, grab/hold (the mod has its own), pack tactics beyond a shared target.

**Can reuse:** the R targetable base's ideas (light, sneak, herd scaling), `HasDirectContact`'s ray approach, emotion
states, `Notify` broadcasting, `GetTraversalCost`, DoT effects on `DamageSource`/`EntityBehaviorHealth`,
`IHarvestableDrops`, entity state tags.
