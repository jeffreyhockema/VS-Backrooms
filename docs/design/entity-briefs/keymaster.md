# The Keymaster: lore and model brief

## Sources
- Wikidot, The Hub: http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/the-hub : overseer of the Hub, antisocial, eager to give a key or guide you to its door so you leave. Links to "The Keymaster" (`trimmed:entity-103`): the entity page has been trimmed (404), so no canon physical description exists on Wikidot.
- Wikidot, Discovery Log (The Hub, offset 1): http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/the-hub/offset/1 . The only physical lines:
  - "slowly descends to the ground, quietly touching down with a faint jingling of his key ring"
  - "stands behind the group, looming tall above them"
  - "spins on his heel and sharply turns around, his cloak swooping with him as he quickly strides down the corridor"
  - "hastily shoves everyone through the door"; "the look in his eyes"; "Yes yes, indeed, goodbye!"
- Wikidot, The Tower (offset 2): http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/the-hub/offset/2 : "quick to point out the door on his own and even guide them to it"; a defaced headless marble statue at the centre.
- Wikidot, Level Keys: http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/object-2 : keys follow 17th-21st c. designs, 3-20 cm, mostly metal, some plastic or stone, themed after their level.
- Miraheze, The Hub: https://backrooms.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Hub : "an imposing marble statue of The Keymaster" in the Tower; wanderers leave offerings. No description of him.
- Fandom (https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/The_Hub) returns 402 to fetches; search summaries add only "Entity X", the sole maker of Level Keys, intelligent and self-aware.

## Keep / change
- KEEP: too tall (43 px, 2.7 blocks), cloak (canon), key ring that jingles (canon), visible eyes (canon), the descend/point/shove/turn/jingle set (each is a canon beat).
- KEEP (not canon, but no canon contradicts it, and it fits "mystery" and the faceless statue): the deep hood, the gaunt pale lower face.
- CHANGE: he "touches down", "spins on his heel" and "strides": give him pointed boots showing under the hem and plant him on the floor; the idle becomes a standing breath, not a 2 px hover bob. In `descend` the toes point down (he is levitating then).
- CHANGE: the keys are a mixed lot, as canon's keys are: iron and brass skeleton keys, short modern keys, one stone, one red plastic.
- CHANGE (quality): solid robe core under the swinging panels (the side view read as two stick legs); a long shoulder cape for the swoop; a real cowl (crown, brim, pointed peak, drape) instead of a cube; bell sleeves; two-joint fingers; the ring moved to the front-left hip, turned 45 degrees so it reads from the front and clears the left arm.
- ADD: `dismiss` (a curt backhand flick: "Yes yes, indeed, goodbye!") and `stride` (a quick walk) for the escort if the code ever walks him.
