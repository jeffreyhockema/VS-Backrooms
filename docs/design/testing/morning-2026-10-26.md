## 2. What the machines found (all fixed unless marked)

1. **Client-only crash at startup** (`Breakdown.FindOwnDrops`: a block type with no drops field has a null Drops array on
   the client). Caught by the rig's first boot; no headless test could see it. Fixed, verified by a real client.
2. **The blackout chat line showed its raw lang key** (`zero-blackout` without the `backrooms:` domain). Caught by the
   rig's blackout scenario. Fixed and re-verified on-screen: "You lose your grip on the place. When you come to, the
   walls are the wrong colour."
3. **Handbook settings bullet lost a space** ("The setting.Backrooms entities"). Fixed.
4. Open, minor: after a blackout the next hunter log line and the telemetry `respawn` event carry the old level for a
   few seconds; a Hound hunt started by the test command with an explicit distance goes to Search at once (the default
   distance hunts normally). Neither affects play.

## 4. Balance numbers worth your eye (nothing changed; these follow the decisions as made)

Felt bodies per hour for a walking target, from the simulated players (Normal):

| Level | Heartland | Approach | Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | 11 | 102 |
| 3 | 15 | 52 | 209 |
| 9 | 0 | 116 | 669 |

The threshold bands of Class 4 and 5 levels are very dense on Normal after the settings shift: Level 9's edge rolls
a body about every 12 seconds (mostly Deathmoth packs), Level 3's every 18. The heart of Level 1 is a body every 7
minutes as decided. If the deep edges feel like a wall rather than a gauntlet, the one-number fix is a floor on any
band's mean interval (for example never under 1 minute), or a larger threshold base than 6 minutes. Recommendation:
floor at 1 minute; decide after a play-test with the telemetry on.

Level 8's felt rate swings tenfold with where the dummy stands (its caves refuse most spots); the spec row is now
informational.

## 6. How to use it from here

- After any merge: `python tools/gate.py --fast` (3.5 min); before a zip: `python tools/gate.py --zip` (50 min).
- A play session: just play; press F8 at any moment worth a note; afterwards
  `python tools/telemetry/report.py <jsonl> --compare docs/design/balance-spec.yaml`.
- A client batch (only when you say): `python tools/clientrig/rig.py --mods <Mods> --scenario tools/clientrig/scenarios/<name>.yaml`;
  the window goes off-screen once the world is in and focus comes back to you.
- A headless session in a level: `python tools/aitest/walk.py --mods <Mods> --level 3 --armour leather --minutes 30`.
