# Progression pressure: decisions

The user's calls of 2026-10-25, so no worker reopens them. See [plan.md](plan.md).

| # | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What carries the progression | **Stability as the depth meter, and lethality that armour answers.** Entities faster than the player deeper, where lore allows: "lucky not to meet them, or prepared to fight", like vanilla's wolves and bears. Not chosen: lock-and-key tool gates; camps and anchors as a mechanism | the user |
| 2 | Hard gates | **Soft gates only.** Level walls **stay breakable**. **Ore only in Level 8** (vanilla deposits off under the other levels; generated chunks keep theirs) | the user |
| 3 | Zero stability | **Hunters come; if it stays at zero you wake at a random place in the previous level**, inventory intact, **no thin spot** | the user: "I like both, but I don't want the thin spot" |
| 4 | Wards | **A worn anchor per tier plus armour**; the temporal part is **vanilla temporal gears, found and bought** | the user |
| 5 | Lethality | **2-3 hits unarmoured at Level 3, 1-2 by Level 8**; leather halves it at Level 3, bronze to stand at Level 8, iron at Level 9 and deeper. **All hunters outrun a sprint from Level 3** except the lore-slow (Wretch, Clump, Duller) | the user |
| 6 | Almond Water | **Diminishing returns deep**, never above the ward tier's ceiling | the user |
| 7 | Unwarded one tier beyond | **About 10 real minutes** to zero; two beyond about 3; at or below the tier no drain | the user |
| 8 | Pace and settings | **8 to 12 real hours to Level 8.** Lethality is not a setting: **the entity setting's names shift up** (today's normal rate becomes low, high becomes normal, very high becomes high, a new top) | the user: today's balance "is low, not normal" |
| 9 | Old worlds, respawn, death, release | New rules apply everywhere, nothing breaks. **Respawn at the last bed slept in** (else Level 0). **Corpses untouched.** **Folded into 0.515.0** | the user |
| 10 | Early leather | **Rat hides and salvaged "pleather"** from cars and hotel upholstery: thin and fragile, its own worse armour, never a substitute for leather in recipes, so tanning stays the real road | the user |
| 11 | Trade | **Wanderers sell ward parts (gears) for rusty gears**, never a finished anchor or armour | the user |
| 12 | Wake spot | **A random place in the previous level** | the user: lore-true noclipping |
| 13 | Level 2's tier (planner) | **Tier 0**, so scrap copper is reachable without a ward; **Level 94 tier 2**, so iron's fire clay is a bronze-tier climax | avoids two circles in the ladder; **confirmed by the user** (Level 2 tier 0; no Level 1 clay source: raids into Level 3 are the point; build it now, folded into 0.515.0) |
| 14 | Set-piece scaling | **Set-pieces scale by the square root of the setting's multiplier** (night visits to a sleeping player, hive waves, the Mangled's fog, the Beast): Normal is about 2.3 times the original values, not 5.5 times | the user, after the first play-test of the shifted names |
| 15 | Blackout cause | **A minute at zero stability blacks you out from any cause**, in every level but Level 0, the Hub, Kitty's House and the Snackrooms | the user |
| 16 | Drain variance | **"About ten minutes" stays about**: lamp-lit calm places (Level 4, the city, the Poolrooms) hold a few minutes longer, dark pockets a little less; two tiers beyond is about three minutes everywhere | the user |
| 17 | Gear price | **A wanderer sells a temporal gear for 6 rusty gears** (about eight mid-level Hound bounties) | the user |
